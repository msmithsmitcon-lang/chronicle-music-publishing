# Chronicle Music Publishing Brand Guide

## Positioning

Chronicle Music Publishing is an independent premium music publishing house. It represents, administers, protects, and develops musical works, catalogues, songwriters, artists, producers, and rights owners. M-WIS is one represented artist and catalogue example within Chronicle, not the sole reason the publishing house exists.

## Tone

- Premium
- Precise
- Rights-aware
- International
- Legacy-minded
- Commercially serious

## Visual Direction

- Primary palette: black, charcoal, gold, ivory.
- Avoid loud entertainment styling, neon club language, or social-first creator branding.
- Use restrained spacing, strong typography, high contrast, and editorial composition.
- Photography should suggest publishing, archives, recording heritage, notation, contracts, catalogue depth, or premium music environments.

## Messaging Pillars

- Music IP stewardship
- Catalogue administration
- Composition rights management
- Royalty registration readiness
- Licensing readiness
- Songwriter and artist publishing representation
- Metadata and rights documentation
- Creator legacy
- International publishing access

## Technology Partner Reference

Chronicle should not use Sentry Sound as part of its public identity. If a technical support reference is needed, use restrained language such as: "Rights workflows may be supported through Sentry Sound platform infrastructure."
