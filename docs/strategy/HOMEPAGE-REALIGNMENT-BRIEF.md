# Homepage Realignment Brief

## Purpose

The homepage must introduce Chronicle Music as an independent music company.

It should not introduce Chronicle Music primarily as:

- a publishing office
- a rights administration company
- a catalogue database
- a metadata operation
- a technology platform

---

## Core Homepage Question

Within 5 seconds a visitor should understand:

- Who Chronicle Music is
- What Chronicle Music does
- Which artists Chronicle Music represents or develops
- Where to discover the music

---

## Homepage Positioning

Primary message:

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music.

Supporting message:

Chronicle Music is building a growing catalogue through artists, releases, and long-term music development.

---

## Recommended Hero Direction

Headline:

Building music that lasts.

Sub-headline:

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists, releasing original music, and growing a catalogue for the future.

Primary CTA:

Explore Our Artists

Secondary CTA:

Discover the Music

---

## Recommended Homepage Hierarchy

1. Hero
2. Featured Artists
3. Latest Releases
4. Selected Music
5. About Chronicle Music
6. Contact / Enquiries

---

## What Must Move Deeper

The homepage should not lead with:

- rights administration
- registrations
- royalty processing
- metadata
- publishing documentation
- catalogue governance
- Sentry Sound references

These may exist deeper in the website.

---

## What Must Become More Visible

- artists
- music
- releases
- discovery
- company identity
- creative development

---

## Success Criteria

A first-time visitor should leave the homepage understanding:

- Chronicle Music is a music company.
- Chronicle Music develops artists.
- Chronicle Music releases original music.
- The artists are the public face.
- The catalogue supports the business.
- Rights and publishing exist, but are not the opening story.
