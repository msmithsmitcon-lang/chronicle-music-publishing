# Chronicle Music Doctrine

## 1. Purpose

Chronicle Music must be understood as an independent music company, not merely a catalogue website, artist page, streaming destination, technology demo, or rights administration brand.

This document defines the public, strategic, and operating doctrine for Chronicle Music.

---

## 2. Core Separation

Chronicle Music, the website, and Sentry Sound must remain clearly separated:

- Chronicle Music = the music business
- Chronicle Music Website = the public-facing brand and discovery layer
- Sentry Sound = the operating system / system of record behind the business

The public does not need to understand Sentry Sound in order to understand Chronicle Music.

---

## 2A. Public vs Strategic Orientation

Chronicle Music is artist-facing publicly and asset-focused strategically.

Publicly, the company should lead with:

- artists
- music
- releases
- discovery
- creative development

Strategically, the company focuses on:

- catalogue growth
- ownership
- publishing opportunities
- licensing opportunities
- long-term music assets

The public should primarily experience artists and music.

The business should internally operate around asset creation, ownership, growth, and long-term value.

This distinction is intentional and must be preserved.

---

## 3. Public Doctrine

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music.

Use this doctrine for:

- website copy
- homepage messaging
- social media bios
- artist introductions
- public communications
- press-style descriptions

Public-facing language should emphasize:

- artists
- music
- releases
- discovery
- originality
- creative development

Public-facing language should avoid leading with:

- rights administration
- metadata
- registrations
- royalty processing
- catalogue governance
- technology infrastructure
- internal operating systems

---

## 4. Strategic Doctrine

Chronicle Music exists to build, manage and grow long-term music assets through artists, releases, catalogue development, publishing and licensing opportunities.

Use this doctrine for:

- business strategy
- internal planning
- investor thinking
- catalogue development
- long-term company direction
- future growth decisions

This doctrine may guide the business, but it should not be used as the homepage headline.

---

## 5. Operating Doctrine

Ownership precedes registration. Registration precedes release. Release precedes distribution. Distribution precedes royalty events.

Use this doctrine for:

- Sentry Sound alignment
- rights workflow
- catalogue governance
- release management
- publishing administration
- operational sequencing

This is an internal operating principle, not public marketing copy.

---

Correct hierarchy:

Chronicle Music

- independent music company

M-Wis

- artist

Huey D

- artist

Future Artists

- artist roster

Chronicle Music is not an artist.

The website must not make Chronicle Music appear to be one artist with many songs.

---

## 7. Website Hierarchy

The public website should follow this priority:

1. Chronicle Music as the company
2. Featured artists
3. Releases / music discovery
4. Selected music / catalogue proof
5. About the company
6. Contact / enquiries

The catalogue is important, but it is not the opening story.

The catalogue should be treated as proof of depth, not the primary identity of the website.

---

## 8. Artist Positioning

Artist pages should present:

- artist identity
- story
- style
- selected releases
- selected music
- enquiry path

Artist pages should not feel like database pages.

---

## 9. Catalogue Positioning

The catalogue should be presented as:

- selected music
- represented works
- catalogue highlights
- music available for discovery, publishing, or licensing discussion

Avoid making the website feel like:

- a giant database
- a rights portal
- a metadata catalogue
- a publishing administration office

---

## 10. Relationship to Sentry Sound

Sentry Sound is the operational system behind the business.

Chronicle Music is the public-facing music company.

Sentry Sound may support:

- ownership tracking
- registration readiness
- release preparation
- distribution management
- royalty event tracking
- evidence management
- catalogue governance

But Chronicle Music should not publicly depend on Sentry Sound as its identity.

---

## 11. Website Decision Rule

When making website decisions, ask:

What does the public need to see?

Do not start with:

How does the company operate internally?

The website should look artist-and-music-facing while the company may operate asset-and-rights-facing internally.

---

## 12. Future Growth Principle

Public layer:

Artists, music, releases, discovery.

Strategic layer:

Catalogue assets, publishing, licensing, ownership, long-term value.

Operating layer:

Ownership, registration, release, distribution, royalty events.

All future Chronicle Music decisions should preserve this separation.
