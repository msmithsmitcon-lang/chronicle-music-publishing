# Chronicle Music Website Positioning Guide

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains how the Chronicle Music website should present the company publicly.

It must follow:

- Public Doctrine
- Strategic Doctrine
- Operating Doctrine

from:

docs/strategy/CHRONICLE-MUSIC-DOCTRINE.md

---

## 2. Website Role

The website is the public-facing discovery layer for Chronicle Music.

It should present Chronicle Music as:

An independent music company developing artists and releasing original music.

The website should not present Chronicle Music primarily as:

- a catalogue database
- a rights administration company
- a publishing administration office
- a technology platform
- a Sentry Sound demonstration site

---

## 3. Homepage Purpose

The homepage should answer within 5 seconds:

- Who is Chronicle Music?
- What does it do?
- Who does it represent?
- Where can visitors discover the music?

Homepage priority:

1. Company identity
2. Featured artists
3. Releases / music discovery
4. Selected music proof
5. About Chronicle Music
6. Contact / enquiries

The homepage should not open with:

- catalogue size
- rights administration
- metadata
- registrations
- internal workflow language

---

## 4. Recommended Homepage Messaging

Primary positioning:

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music.

Suggested hero direction:

Headline:

Building music that lasts.

Sub-headline:

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists, releasing original music, and growing a catalogue for the future.

Primary CTA:

Explore Our Artists

Secondary CTA:

Discover the Music

---

## 5. Navigation Purpose

Recommended navigation:

Home
Artists
Music
Releases
About
Contact

Optional future navigation:

Licensing
News

Avoid making Catalogue the dominant navigation label unless it is framed carefully.

Preferred public wording:

Music
Selected Music
Music Catalogue
Catalogue Highlights

Avoid:

Database
Metadata
Rights Portal
Publishing Admin
Registration Dashboard

---

## 6. Artists Page Purpose

Artists pages should present the roster.

Purpose:

- show who Chronicle Music represents or develops
- introduce artist identity
- guide visitors into music discovery
- support future roster growth

Artist pages should not feel like database pages.

---

## 7. Artist Detail Page Purpose

Artist detail pages should include:

- artist story
- sound/style
- selected releases
- selected music
- enquiry path

They should make clear:

- Chronicle Music is the company
- M-Wis, Huey D, and future roster members are artists

---

## 8. Music / Catalogue Purpose

Music and catalogue content should act as proof of depth.

Catalogue visibility should support:

- discovery
- listening
- artist credibility
- licensing or publishing conversations

It should not dominate the public identity.

Use:

- selected works
- represented works
- catalogue highlights
- music discovery

Avoid:

- full database language
- metadata-heavy language
- review pending language
- rights administration language

---

## 9. Releases Purpose

Releases should become the public-facing proof of activity.

Purpose:

- show current music activity
- highlight artists
- create social-media-friendly moments
- support public promotion

Releases should feel more public-facing than catalogue records.

---

## 10. About Page Purpose

The About page should explain Chronicle Music as:

- independent music company
- artist and catalogue development company
- long-term music business

It can lightly reference catalogue growth and licensing opportunities, but should not become a rights administration page.

---

## 11. Contact Page Purpose

The Contact page should support:

- artist enquiries
- publishing / licensing conversations
- collaboration opportunities
- general contact

Use clear public language.

Avoid internal operational language.

---

## 12. Sentry Sound Separation

Sentry Sound may support Chronicle Music operationally, but Chronicle Music's website should not depend on Sentry Sound messaging.

Do not make public pages feel like:

- Sentry Sound demo pages
- rights workflow dashboards
- registration tools
- metadata systems

---

## 13. Website Decision Rule

Before changing a website page, ask:

Does this help the public understand Chronicle Music as an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music?

If yes, proceed.

If it mainly exposes internal operations, rights administration, metadata, registration, or Sentry Sound infrastructure, keep it out of the public website or move it deeper into appropriate context.
