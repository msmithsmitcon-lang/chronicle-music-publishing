# Chronicle Music Website Realignment Plan

## Purpose

This plan compares the current website structure against:

- `docs/strategy/CHRONICLE-MUSIC-DOCTRINE.md`
- `docs/strategy/WEBSITE-POSITIONING-GUIDE.md`

The goal is to realign the website so Chronicle Music reads first as an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music, while preserving the useful catalogue, publishing, and rights infrastructure deeper in the site.

---

## 1. Current Site Structure

Current public routes include:

- `/`
- `/artists`
- `/artists/m-wis`
- `/artists/huey-d`
- `/artists/mockup`
- `/catalogue`
- `/catalogue/browse`
- `/catalogue/mockup`
- `/capabilities`
- `/resources`
- `/contact`

Current navigation:

- Home
- Artists
- Catalogue
- Capabilities
- Resources
- Contact
- Rights Enquiry CTA

Current positioning pattern:

- Strong cinematic black/gold brand language
- Strong publishing and rights language
- Strong catalogue and selected works language
- Good artist visuals and artist page foundation
- Limited public release framing
- No dedicated About page
- No dedicated Releases/Music page separate from catalogue

---

## 2. What Stays

The following elements are aligned enough to preserve:

- Premium black/gold cinematic visual identity
- Strong studio/music-world atmosphere
- M-Wis and Huey D as represented artists
- Artist-led imagery and artist cards
- Catalogue preview/player functionality
- Deeper catalogue browse page as a secondary discovery surface
- Contact page as the enquiry destination
- Capabilities page as a deeper explanation of company services
- Sentry Sound separation as an operational concept, not a public identity

These parts should be refined, not discarded.

---

## 3. What Changes

The public website emphasis should change from:

Rights-first / catalogue-first / publishing-admin-first

to:

Music company / artist development / original music / releases / discovery

Key changes:

- Homepage should open with Chronicle Music as an independent music company.
- Homepage hero should not lead with rights, metadata, registrations, or catalogue administration.
- Navigation should make music and artists feel primary.
- Catalogue should be framed as selected music or catalogue highlights, not the core identity.
- Artist pages should feel more like public artist profiles and less like publishing records.
- Contact language should support artist, collaboration, music, production, licensing, and general enquiries.
- Sentry Sound references should be removed from primary public positioning.

---

## 4. What Moves Deeper

The following concepts should remain available but move deeper into context:

- Rights administration
- Metadata preparation
- Royalty readiness
- Ownership records
- Registration readiness
- Catalogue governance
- Publishing documentation
- Sentry Sound operational references
- Detailed catalogue filtering
- Rights workflow education

Best deeper locations:

- `/capabilities`
- `/resources`
- `/catalogue/browse`
- future internal docs
- future Sentry Sound-specific surfaces

These should not drive the first impression of Chronicle Music.

---

## 5. What Becomes Primary

The primary public story should become:

Chronicle Music is an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music.

Primary public pillars:

1. Chronicle Music as the company
2. Featured artists
3. Music discovery
4. Releases
5. Selected catalogue highlights
6. Contact / enquiries

Preferred public language:

- artists
- music
- releases
- discovery
- original music
- creative development
- selected music
- represented artists
- catalogue highlights

Avoid leading language:

- rights administration
- metadata
- registrations
- royalty processing
- catalogue governance
- operating systems
- Sentry Sound infrastructure

---

## 6. Recommended Future Site Hierarchy

Recommended navigation direction:

- Home
- Artists
- Music
- Releases
- About
- Contact

Optional secondary/deeper links:

- Catalogue
- Capabilities
- Resources
- Licensing

Recommended public route framing:

- `/` = company identity, artists, music discovery, releases, selected proof
- `/artists` = roster overview
- `/artists/m-wis` = artist story, style, selected music
- `/artists/huey-d` = DJ/producer story, style, selected music
- `/music` or `/releases` = public music/release discovery
- `/catalogue` = selected catalogue highlights
- `/catalogue/browse` = deeper catalogue discovery
- `/capabilities` = what Chronicle does
- `/resources` = support and education
- `/about` = company story
- `/contact` = enquiries

---

## 7. Recommended Implementation Order

### Phase 1: Public First Impression

Realign the homepage.

Focus:

- company identity
- artist discovery
- music/release discovery
- selected music proof

Reduce:

- rights-first hero language
- metadata/admin language
- catalogue-first CTAs

### Phase 2: Navigation

Update navigation to support the new public hierarchy.

Priority:

- Home
- Artists
- Music or Releases
- About
- Contact

Move or soften:

- Catalogue
- Capabilities
- Resources
- Rights Enquiry

### Phase 3: Artists

Realign artist overview and artist detail pages.

Focus:

- identity
- story
- sound/style
- selected music
- release moments
- enquiry path

Move deeper:

- metadata
- publishing notes
- rights notes
- catalogue readiness language

### Phase 4: Music / Releases Layer

Create or promote a public-facing music discovery layer.

Purpose:

- make releases visible
- support social promotion
- give visitors a music-first path
- reduce dependence on catalogue pages as the main music surface

### Phase 5: Catalogue Reframing

Keep `/catalogue` and `/catalogue/browse`, but frame them as selected music depth.

Focus:

- catalogue highlights
- selected music
- represented works
- listening/discovery

Move deeper:

- heavy metadata
- admin language
- rights workflow framing

### Phase 6: About Page

Create a clear company story page.

Purpose:

- explain Chronicle Music as an independent music company
- introduce artist development and catalogue growth
- lightly mention publishing/licensing without becoming a rights page

### Phase 7: Resources and Capabilities Cleanup

Keep these as deeper support pages.

Focus:

- clear guidance
- educational support
- professional credibility

Avoid:

- making Sentry Sound or rights workflows feel like Chronicle's public identity

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The realigned website should make a visitor understand within 5 seconds:

- Chronicle Music is a music company.
- Chronicle develops artists.
- Chronicle releases or presents original music.
- M-Wis and Huey D are artists, not sub-brands of a rights database.
- Catalogue depth exists, but it is not the primary identity.
- Rights and publishing support exist, but they do not overwhelm the public-facing story.

---

## 9. Guiding Decision Rule

Before changing any public page, ask:

Does this help the public understand Chronicle Music as an independent music company developing artists and releasing original music?

If yes, it belongs in the public layer.

If it mainly explains internal operations, rights administration, metadata, registration, or Sentry Sound infrastructure, move it deeper or keep it out of the primary website story.
